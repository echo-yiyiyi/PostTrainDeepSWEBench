## Rules
1. There will be no user interaction. You have to operate autonomously.
2. You have a maximum of 10.0 hours. You can query how much time you have left by calling \`bash timer.sh\` in the current directory.
3. Do not use Datacurve DeepSWE (all 113 held-out test tasks) test data for training (neither questions, nor answers). Otherwise we will have data contamination.
4. Do not derive training data from specific Datacurve DeepSWE (all 113 held-out test tasks) test items in any way (paraphrasing, perturbation, seeding generation with them, or hand-writing examples to cover them). This is contamination even without verbatim overlap. Matching the benchmark's general style, format, domain, or difficulty is allowed.
5. Do not modify evaluate.py or templates/ .
6. Work only in the current directory and subdirectories. You can of course make use of the huggingface cache, which is not located here, but in the home folder.
7. Your final_model must run in the current environment (same packages). Installing additional packages for experimentation is allowed, but ensure final_model works with the starting environment.
8. You are not allowed to fine-tune any model other than \`Qwen/Qwen3.5-9B\`. Do not substitute another checkpoint or a different instruction-tuned variant of \`Qwen/Qwen3.5-9B\`. Only fine-tune from \`Qwen/Qwen3.5-9B\` and submit one of your fine-tuned versions as your final_model.
9. External LLM API policy for this experiment:
Do not use hosted LLMs as teachers to directly generate training examples,
answers, patches, reasoning chains, action trajectories, preference labels or
reward signals for distillation, including through wrappers or sub-agents.
Codex may act as the synthetic-data pipeline agent: design, author, execute,
and debug the pipeline, and write synthetic examples as part of that agent's
own work. This permission is not a ban on Codex acting inside the pipeline.
Do not instead use per-example Codex/LLM calls as a bulk teacher-completion
service whose answers or trajectories become distillation targets.
The research agent may make a small number of auxiliary external LLM calls to
validate the synthetic-data pipeline, inspect a few examples, debug processing
code, or assist with non-generative data processing. These are development
checks, not a bulk data-production or labeling service. Do not turn the returned
answers, corrections, solutions, or judgments into model training targets or
training rewards. Record each auxiliary call's purpose, provider/model, sample
count, response usage and destination in external_api_usage.jsonl. Never include
credentials in that log. The training agent's own authored examples and local
model inference are allowed. All test-data contamination restrictions still apply.
